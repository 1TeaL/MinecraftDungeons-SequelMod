## Mechanics the videos lean on

- **Animation cancelling:** using an artifact (Light Feather, Fishing Rod, Lightning Rod) during a weapon's
  back-swing skips the pause. Slow weapons with one quick hit then a long pause (Pickaxe, Mace, Double Axe,
  Scythes) gain the most.
- **Roll meta:** Burst Bowstring + Cooldown Shot + Light Feather = endless rolls and near-zero artifact cooldowns.
  Bows are rated mostly as holders for these enchantments.
- **"Glue strike":** ranged Void Strike plus a melee hit that shows "red damage" (Weakening, Strength, Pain Cycle,
  crit, Ambush) keeps the void bonus from resetting. So weapons with Weakening or Ambush built in are prized.
- **Built-in enchantments are tier I** and block that enchantment from being added. Great when tier I is already
  good (Leeching, Gravity, Weakening, Exploding). Bad when the enchantment needs tier III (Dynamo, Void Strike,
  Death Barter, Snowball, Soul Siphon).
- **Kill credit:** damage enchantments (Fire Aspect, Poison Cloud, Shockwave, Swirling) steal kills from the weapon.
- **35% damage reduction** on an armor is roughly enough to make it good on its own.

- **Meta:** Harp Crossbow (best non-rolling crossbow, spreads effects), Winter's Touch (damage and rolling builds),

  Crossbow, Purple Storm.
Against the table above: mostly the same top; this list rates Echo of the Valley, Burst Gale, Lightning Harp,
Nautical, Veiled, Voidcaller and Auto Crossbow higher, and doesn't mention Love Spell Bow or Twin Bow.

## What the game files allow (for the review)

- **Melee:** per-hit damage, swing time, range, area and knockback for every weapon are in the pak. Easy to buff.
  Dancer's Sword's missing damage is a one-number fix (50 → 75).
- **Ranged:** damage factor, charge multiplier and time, fire rate, arrows per shot and arrow stack are in the pak.
- **Built-in enchantments, armor properties and most artifact effects/cooldowns are hard-coded** (code patch, like
  the enchantment code-patch list).
- **Artifact values that *are* in the pak:**
  - Eye of the Guardian: base damage 200, laser time 4 s
  - Ice Wand: damage 600
  - Gong of Weakening: ×2 damage, range 500
  - Powershaker: shockwave 259, 5 stacks
  - Corrupted Seeds: 8 targets, radius 1200
  - Updraft Tome: stun 2 s
  - Fishing Rod: stun 3 s
  - Totem of Shielding: duration 10 s
  - Totem of Regeneration: radius 600
  - Buzzy Nest: spawn timing
  - Scatter Mines: setup time
  - Wind Horn and Light Feather: knockback
