Artifacts video (xHI7nURpEfU, 64 min, with the Mantis). Three ratings each: early game (Default -> unlocking A+), max Apocalypse, Tower.
Four tiers (S/A/B/C style). Final lists:
- Early game best: Corrupted Beacon, Death Cap Mushroom, Fireworks Arrow, Lightning Rod, Scatter Mines, Power Shaker.
- Max Apocalypse best: Iron Hide Amulet, Love Medallion, Light Feather, Corrupted Seeds, Fishing Rod, Torment Quiver, Gong of Weakening,
  Boots of Swiftness (speedrun), Satchel of Elixirs, Power Shaker, Shadow Shifter, Tome of Duplication, Wind Horn.
- Tower best: Buzzy Nest, Love Medallion, Blast Fungus, Corrupted Seeds, Lightning Rod, Gong of Weakening, Scatter Mines, Power Shaker,
  Shadow Shifter, Wind Horn.

Per artifact:
- Corrupted Beacon: great early, falls off at max (can't boost, can't attack while beaming), bad in Tower (souls). Outclassed by Harvester late.
- Harvester (40 souls): weaker early, scales better at max (blast while attacking/rolling). Not Tower.
- Iron Hide Amulet (50% dmg reduction): permanent by ~power 200 with one cooldown enchant; near universal at max. Weak in Tower.
- Death Cap Mushroom (+100% attack speed, +20% move): best early; good at max but outclassed by animation-cancel artifacts.
- Light Feather (3 s cooldown roll + stun): weak early/Tower; core of roll meta at max.
- Tasty Bone (wolf): good early, outclassed at max (one pet), meh Tower.
- Golem Kit: like wolf; better in Tower.
- Wonderful Wheat (llama): low damage, ranged pet draws no aggro; weak everywhere.
- Buzzy Nest (hive, 3 bees): mid early, ok at max, must-have in Tower.
- Soul Lantern (60 souls, wizard 30 s): "not really viable anywhere".
- Enchanted Grass (random sheep): "completely unviable", bad.
- Vexing Chant (3 vexes, decay): great damage early (hard to get), OK at max (3 beast bursts), good Tower.
- Love Medallion (3 mobs as allies 10 s): inefficient early; best pet artifact at max and Tower; no enchanted mobs/bosses.
- Blast Fungus: good everywhere, especially Tower (knockback).
- Spin Blade: good early/Tower, harder at max (not buffable).
- Eye of the Guardian: "dog water" - locks you, slow turn, 1/4 beacon damage, 22 s cooldown not reduced by burst shots. Terrible everywhere.
- Corrupted Seeds (entangle 8 + poison): mid early, great at max and Tower.
- Fishing Rod: 1.5 s cooldown -> animation-cancel tool at max; weak as an effect.
- Flaming Quiver (5 fire arrows): nice early, falls off hard at max, steals kills.
- Fireworks Arrow (1 arrow, explodes): great early, still OK at max with multi-arrow bows.
- Torment Quiver (10 souls, 3 arrows): weak early/Tower; at max = infinite souls with Soul Siphon. Widest late niche.
- Thundering Quiver (5 arcing arrows): a better Flaming Quiver; usable everywhere.
- Harpoon Quiver (5 piercing harpoons): "generally good at all stages".
- Void Quiver (5 void arrows): outclassed early/late (just enchant void strike); useful in Tower.
- Lightning Rod (15 souls, boosted by lightning focus not soul focus): S early and Tower; awkward at max.
- Gong of Weakening (x2 damage taken, weakening): great everywhere.
- Ice Wand (ice block, stun): can't aim -> "eh" everywhere.
- Shock Powder (stun nearby): good early; at max good only with cooldown spam; OK Tower.
- Boots of Swiftness: speedrun item; not defensive; bad Tower.
- Ghost Cloak (speed + ghost form 50%): outclassed by boots early, low uptime, good Tower escape.
- Totem of Regeneration: good early (esp. multiplayer), falls off hard at max, OK Tower.
- Totem of Shielding (blocks projectiles): niche (Arch-Illager fight, head-placement glitch).
- Totem of Soul Protection? (not covered) / Totem of Casting (70 souls, refresh cooldowns): "terrible" everywhere.
- Satchel of Elements (7 mobs: freeze/fire/lightning): good, lightning ~ lightning rod each; good Tower.
- Satchel of Elixirs (2 random potions, 25 s): great everywhere; power-independent.
- Satchel of Snacks (1 food, 16 s): weak early/max (Food Reserves better); good Tower.
- Scatter Mines (3 mines, huge damage, can't aim): great early/Tower, strong at max (steals kills).
- Updraft Tome (launch 7, auto-target, 12 s): good early/Tower; worse at max (charge time resets).
- Power Shaker (next 5 hits explode, chains): great everywhere.
- Enchanter's Tome: great multiplayer only; bad solo (only pets).
- Shadow Shifter (40 souls, shadow form 8x): weak early; overpowered max and Tower.
- Soul Healer (50 souls, small heal): bad everywhere at max; meh early.
- Tome of Duplication (40 souls, copy last consumable): bad early/Tower; at max prints arrow bundles for burst bowstring.
- Wind Horn (push + 75% slow): very strong (creator corrected earlier low ranking).
Not covered / unknown: Soul Lantern above, Totem of Spirit Protection? Iron hide etc. Others from DLC: Cogs? Mob Masher, Encrusted? (check list)
