Bows ranked assuming Burst Bowstring + Cooldown Shot ("everything is broken with it"): Frown bow = only a cooldown-shot holder.

Bows:
- Bonebow (Bow, growing): average; Frown.
- Twin Bow (Bow, bonus shot): fake arrow -> dynamo glitch; Smile.
- Bubble Burster (reliable ricochet + bubbles 40% charged dmg): Deface.
- Ancient Bow (Hunting Bow, dynamo T1): T1 dynamo worse than enchanting it; pet targeting useless. Frown.
- Hunter's Promise (Hunting Bow, infinity): +6 arrows meh. Frown.
- Master's Bow (Hunting Bow, power): 6.28 dmg, outclassed. Frown (best of the hunting bows).
- Guardian Bow (Longbow, supercharge): 7.93 dmg, low ammo; outclassed by power bows. Frown-ish.
- Red Snake (Longbow, fuse shot): same issue. Frown-ish.
- Elite Power Bow (Power Bow, power): 13.33 dmg (highest), 13 ammo; overcharge x8. Smile/strong for damage.
- Sabrewing (Power Bow, radiance shot T1): outclassed by Elite. Smile-ish.
- Love Spell Bow (Shortbow, wild rage): low dmg, 80 ammo; one of best rolling bows. Deface.
- Mechanical Shortbow (Shortbow, accelerate): quick charge + accelerate, fastest; overcharge at normal charge time. Deface.
- Purple Storm (Shortbow, rapid fire): worse than siblings, still high ammo. Smile.
- Winter's Touch (3 real arrows per charged shot, 16.7 effective): Deface; best damage bow.
- Weeping Vine Bow (poison trail? roll charge T1): average dmg, worst ammo (10). Frown "this thing sucks".
- Green Menace (reliable ricochet + poison cloud): Deface.
- Pink Scoundrel (reliable ricochet + wild rage): Deface, better than Green Menace.
- Call of the Void (fuse shot + void strike T1): niche glue strike; Smile (would drop to Flat).
- Burst Gale Bow (Wind Bow, roll charge): pulls mobs; Smile.
- Echo of the Valley (Wind Bow, ricochet): Smile.
- Bow of Lost Souls (Soul, multishot): strong support. Smile/Deface.
- Nocturnal Bow (Soul, tempo theft): best support bow at max Apocalypse. Deface-ish.
Best: Winter's Touch (dmg), Nocturnal Bow (support), Bubble Burster / Pink Scoundrel (mix).
