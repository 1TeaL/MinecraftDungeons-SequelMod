# Player sentiment on the base game (web search, 2026-10-19)

Summarized in my own words from reviews, the official feedback site and forums (Reddit blocked, not used).

| Complaint | What players / reviewers said | Mod's answer |
|---|---|---|
| Endgame grind | Rerolling enchantments and shop stock priced to punish; emeralds, gold and gilded items slow on purpose; gear goes out of date with no other permanent progress | Rerolls at a tenth, Piglin gilded half price, gold from mobs past A+25, power past the cap unlocks enchantments, perks and the A+100 ladder |
| Apocalypse Plus | Steep spikes from about A+18: too many enchanted mobs, too much damage and speed, only exact builds work | Deflect and Levitation Shot gone from mobs; healing, knockback handed back past A+38; weak gear lifted |
| Random shops | Currency only buys random pulls, no saving for a specific item; vendors sell the wrong class and below your level | Stock follows your power level; cheaper rerolls (still random) |
| Drop RNG | At the mercy of random drops, no crafting; Tower loot repeats | Seasonal items in missions and shops; gilded drops; Blacksmith in one mission |
| Repetition | Repetitive enemies and midgame; uneven difficulty | Trials, Endless levels; not addressed: new enemies or levels |
| Multiplayer | Loot can't be shared or traded between players | Not addressed |
| Inventory | 300 items, a fixed number in the game's code (storage chest added in Season 2) | Not possible (would need a code change) |

Mojang's own "Adjusting the Endgame" dev blog admits many missions didn't feel rewarding before the A+ rework.
