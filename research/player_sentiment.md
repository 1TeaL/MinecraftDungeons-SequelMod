# Player sentiment on the base game (web search, 2026-10-19)

Summarized in my own words from reviews, the official feedback site and forums (Reddit blocked, not used).

| Complaint | What players / reviewers said | Mod's answer |
|---|---|---|
| Endgame grind | Rerolling enchantments and shop stock priced to punish; emeralds, gold and gilded items slow on purpose; gear goes out of date with no other permanent progress | Rerolls at a tenth, Piglin gilded half price, gold from mobs past A+25, power past the cap unlocks enchantments, perks and the A+100 ladder |
| Apocalypse Plus | Steep spikes from about A+18: too many enchanted mobs, too much damage and speed, only exact builds work | Deflect and Levitation Shot gone from mobs; healing, knockback handed back past A+38; weak gear lifted |
| Random shops | Currency only buys random pulls, no saving for a specific item; vendors sell the wrong class and below your level | Stock follows your power level; cheaper rerolls (still random) |
| Drop RNG | At the mercy of random drops, no crafting; Tower loot repeats | Seasonal items in missions and shops; gilded drops; Blacksmith in one mission |
| Repetition | Repetitive enemies and midgame; uneven difficulty | Trials, Endless levels; not addressed: new enemies or levels |
| Multiplayer | Loot can't be shared or traded between players (launch reviews) | Answered by the base game itself: the Gift Wrapper (Sept 2020) |
| Inventory | 300 items, a fixed number in the game's code (storage chest added in Season 2) | Not possible (would need a code change) |

Mojang's own "Adjusting the Endgame" dev blog admits many missions didn't feel rewarding before the A+ rework.

## Quality-of-life added to the base game over its updates (web search, 2026-10-19)

- 1.4.3.0 (Sept 2020): Blacksmith (upgrade gear over three missions), **Gift Wrapper** (give an item to a friend
  in the same session, 100-250 emeralds; one gift per session; the item's power is re-rolled for the receiver's
  recommended difficulty, its enchantments are removed, and it can't be gifted again), Luxury and Mystery
  merchants, daily trials, map legend, private games.
- 1.8.0.0: Apocalypse Plus reworked - the old 20 levels squeezed into 10, 15 new ones (25 in all); Blacksmith
  upgrade thresholds tied to A+ levels.
- 1.12.0.0 (Dec 2021): the Tower (30 floors, layout changes every few weeks).
- 1.14.1.0 (Apr 2022): **Storage Chest** in camp (300 more items per character, carried across platforms by cloud
  saves); the "Uniquesmith" matches a chosen item's power to your strongest gear.
- 1.16.2.0 (Oct 2022): Tower multiplayer; **Enchantsmith** (re-roll enchantments for gold, rising cost per roll);
  loot goes to the Storage Chest when the inventory is full; inventory filters on the chest and Blacksmith.
- 1.17.0.0 (Nov 2022, the last update): weekly Tower rotation, seasonal gear as Tower rewards, Enchantsmith can
  replace an enchantment with a stackable one, cheaper Enchantsmith and more gold from Ancient Hunt.
So "loot can't be shared" (launch reviews) was answered by the Gift Wrapper in Sept 2020.
