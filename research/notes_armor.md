Armor video (sKZVlqmRFgI, 82 min, with the Mantis). Uniques only; groups by complexity (plain pasta -> marinara -> parmesan -> hot sauce
-> "pineapple pizza" = the bad ones). Faces: Deface (S) > Smile > Flat > Frown. Key facts: 35% damage reduction modifier alone ~ Smile;
built-in enchants are tier 1 (often a downgrade); rolling/animation cancel meta; armor swapping (Hero's / Troubadour potion cooldown).

- Renegade Armor (melee attack speed, 35% DR, 20% weapon aura): Deface; maybe best beginner armor.
- Full Metal Armor (30% melee dmg, 35% DR, 30% negate, +100% roll cooldown): Deface.
- Frost Armor (35% DR, chilling T1, freeze resist): Flat - only because Full Metal does it better.
- Titan's Shroud (weapon aura, DR, +50% souls): Smile.
- Stalwart Armor (35% DR, 30% negate, potion barrier T1): Deface, "king of damage reduction".
- Guard's Armor (-40% artifact cooldown, +10 arrows): Flat (one real modifier). Curious Armor (unused, 5% teleport on hit).
- Rugged Climbing Gear (-40% cooldown, burn/freeze/knockback resist): Flat.
- Goat Gear (-40% cooldown, multi-roll T1): Flat (a bit better).
- Archer's Armor? "Archer's" (15% move speed aura, +30% ranged dmg): Flat - ranged % rarely matters.
- Wither Armor (DR, souls, life steal aura 6%): Deface; "too overtuned", maybe strongest armor.
- Spider Armor (life steal, attack speed): Deface.
- Fox Armor (30% negate, 20% weapon aura, potions heal allies): Flat (multiplayer tank).
- Highland Armor (swift footed T1, DR, 30% melee): Deface, underrated.
- Hero's Armor (35% DR, -40% potion cooldown): Deface (potion barrier + armor swap).
- Troubadour's Armor (+30% positive status duration, -30% negative): Smile (high).
- Ember Robe (-40% cooldown, 15% move aura, burning T1 = arguably a nerf): Smile (speedrun).
- Verdant Robe (-40% cooldown, move aura, bag of souls): Smile (speedrun).
- Gilded Glory (death barter T1 = 150 emeralds, lucky explorer T1, melee attack speed): Flat/near-bad - T1 built-ins ruin it.
- Splendid Robe (+50% artifact dmg, -40% cooldown, +30% melee): Deface.
- Soul Dancer Robe (30% negate, artifact dmg, souls): Smile.
- Beehive Armor (35% DR, 30% bee on hit): Smile (pet/beast burst niche).
- Nimble Turtle Armor (35% DR, +25% healing, rush T1): Flat - 25% healing too small.
- Sturdy Shulker Armor (deflect T1 25%, 50% DR when swarmed, shulker projectiles): Smile (needs 4 mobs).
- Golden Piglin Armor (artifact dmg, surprise gift T1, reset cooldowns on potion): Flat (anti-synergy).
- Living Vines Armor (acrobat T1 15%, snares on roll): Smile (shadow anchor).
- Opulent Armor (lucky explorer, melee speed, invulnerable on emerald pickup): Deface.
- Ghost Kindler (ghost form on roll = 50% DR, 30% negate, fire on roll): Deface.
- Mystery Armor (2 random modifiers +maybe a negative): Deface (can be anything; grind nightmare).
- Shadow Walker (brief invulnerability when rolling, 35% DR): Deface.
- Glow Squid Armor (brief invuln roll, 15% move aura, ink cloud blinds): Deface - "best armor" for many.
- Unstable Robes (roll to teleport, roll explosion 80% slow, souls): Smile (high); teleport doesn't trigger on Light Feather.

- Black Spot Armor? (food reserves, reset cooldown on potion, potions heal allies): Frown.
- Cave Crawler (artifact dmg, 20% weapon aura, pet bat): Frown (anti-synergy, outclassed by Splendid/Soul Dancer).
- Black Wolf Armor (15% roll cooldown, 20% weapon aura, potions heal allies): Frown, "worst armor".
