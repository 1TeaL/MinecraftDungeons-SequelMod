Melee video (scD0-3_jTdY, 105 min, with "the Mantis" Discord standardized tier list). Damage scaled to weakest (Beestinger=1);
median damage 4.58, median hits/10 s 18.65. Verdict: Smile (strong) / Flat (mediocre) / Frown (bad).
Mechanics: animation cancelling (artifact during back-swing; Light Feather / Fishing Rod / Lightning Rod), "glue strike"
(ranged void + red damage from Weakening/Strength/Pain Cycle/crit/Ambush/shadow), kill credit, additive vs multiplicative.

- Hawk Brand (Sword, crit T1): dmg 3.51, 24.3 hits. Flat. Slightly better than Diamond Sword (crit > sharpness).
- Diamond Sword (Sword, sharpness): same stats. Flat.
- Hammer of Gravity (Great Hammer, gravity): 5.86 / 11.3, great AoE. Smile - gravity built in = free slot.
- Stormlander (Great Hammer, thundering): flat damage thundering on a slow weapon = wasted. Worse than Hammer of Gravity.
- Firebrand (Axe, fire aspect): 4.48 / 16.3, tiny awkward area; fire aspect steals kills. Frown - "maybe the worst melee".
- Highland Axe (Axe, stunning T1): same, stunning never procs on slow weapon. Frown.
- Whirlwind (Double Axe, shockwave): 5.86 / 16.3, great 360 two-hit area; shockwave steals kills/breaks void. Smile.
- Cursed Axe (Double Axe, exploding): best standard melee, S tier. Plain Double Axe also strong.
- Master's Katana (Katana, crit): 5.84 / 18.3, 3-hit, big knockback finisher. Smile (fan favourite).
- Dark Katana (Katana, smiting): same; smiting only undead. Good but behind Master's.
- Battle Staff of Terror (Staff, exploding): 5.13, above-avg speed, terrible single-target area, 20-hit combo. Good but high skill.
- Growing Staff (Staff, committed): same. Slightly behind Terror.
- Venom Glaive (Glaive, poison cloud): long reach but only 3rd hit sweeps; "mediocre, kind of useless" except Power Shaker builds.
- Grave Bane (Glaive, smiting): same, mediocre.
- Diamond Pickaxe (Pickaxe, prospector): 7.79 / 14.3, single target, great cancel potential. Smile ("very strong").
- Sun's Grace (Mace, radiance): 6.82 / 15.7, stubby area; only good with auto-clicker. A tier.
- Flail (Mace, chains): same; better without auto-clicker. A tier.
- Nightmare's Bite (Sickle, poison cloud): very low damage, fast, poison steals. Frown "wimpy".
- Last Laugh (Sickle, prospector): Frown, "sickles kind of suck".
- Fangs of Frost (Daggers, freezing): low damage, poor area, 6-hit combo. Frown-ish ("kind of suck").
- Sheer Daggers (Daggers, swirling): steals kills; best with thundering + glue strike. Frown-ish.
- Moon Daggers (Daggers, enigma resonator, soul): 43 hits/10 s soul weapon -> Soul Siphon king. Smile.
- Broadsword (Claymore, sharpness): above-avg dmg, slow, awkward first hit. Weak - outclassed by Heartstealer.
- Great Axeblade (Claymore, dynamo T1): T1 dynamo pointless. Weak.
- Heartstealer (Claymore, leeching): T1 leeching great -> free slot. Smile.
- Dancer's Sword (Cutlass, rampaging T1): 2.93 dmg. MISSED the Cutlass +50% damage buff (bug). Frown - "never use".
- Nameless Blade (Cutlass, weakening): 4.39, fast; weakening = free glue strike. S tier.
- Maulers (Gauntlets, rampaging T1): very low dmg, not fast. Frown - "truly garbage".
- Soul Fists (Gauntlets, soul): strictly worse than Moon Daggers. Flat.
- Fighting Bindings (Gauntlets, critical? stun): 65 hits/10 s, nerfed; shockwave/swirling builds overrated. Flat / B tier.
- Beestinger (Rapier, busy bee): damage 1 (lowest), 56 hits; busy bee never procs (no kills). Niche - Power Shaker. OK.
- Freezing Foil (Rapier, freezing): attacks slower than Beestinger. OK/niche (creator jokingly "terrible").
- Mechanized Sawblade: 2.55 / 70.3 hits, "dynamo chaining" bug. S tier, "broken".
- Truthseeker (Soul Knife, committed): 8.28 / 11.3, slow for soul enchants. Good.
- Eternal Knife (Soul Knife, soul siphon T1): T1 soul siphon useless. Behind Truthseeker.
- Jailor's Scythe (Soul Scythe, chains): 4.69 / 9.3?, 180 area, passive souls. Strong.
- Frost Scythe (Soul Scythe, freezing): same, freezing better. Strong.
- Resolute Tempest Knife (Tempest Knife, committed + rushdown): weaker than standard weapons, small area. Flat "not impressive".
- Chill Gale Knife (Tempest Knife, freezing): worse than Resolute. Flat/weak.
- Fortune Spear (Spear, looting): low dmg & speed but gravity at spear tip = niche utility. Good.
- Whispering Spear (Spear, echo): echo = attack speed here; slightly better. Good.
- The Beginning and the End (Void Touched Blades, leeching + void strike T1): T1 void strike blocks T3 and breaks glue strike. Not strong.
- Sponge Striker (Coral Blade): 1.61 / 35; useless modifier. Frown - "terrible".
- Vine Whip (Whip, jungle poison): poison scales with hit (dynamo, void) -> broken boss killer. S.
- Encrusted Anchor (Anchor, jungle poison + gravity): 14 dmg, huge area, 4 hits/10 s. Best weapon in game.
- Swift Striker (Swift Striker? End, ambush + echo): low dmg, low area. Flat.
- Bone Cudgel (Bone Club, illager's bane): ~10 dmg, slow, 180 two-hit, great knockback. Strong.
- Starless Night (Obsidian Claymore, shared pain): 13.14 avg, 2nd hit massive. Smile/top, easiest.
Mantis tier list terrible: Maulers, Firebrand, Highland Axe, Sponge Striker.
