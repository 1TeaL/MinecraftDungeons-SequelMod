# Bonus enchantments: every item different (applied 2026-11-12)

Rule (user): a plain item may share its bonuses with **one** of its uniques; two uniques of the same item must
differ, and so must different items. "Gets now" = the bonuses the item really ends up with past power 316 (its list's
first ones it doesn't already own). **Bold** = the change. Items not listed keep their lists.
