# Community opinions - "Ranking EVERY Enchantment in Minecraft Dungeons!"

Source: https://youtu.be/VfrkMHXG6Ak (86 min, recent - mentions Minecraft Dungeons 2).
Summarized in my own words from the auto-generated captions. The creator ranks for general play
(default -> max Apocalypse); a second list by expert endgame players is noted where it differs.

Verdict scale used here: **Top** > **Strong** > **Good** > **Meh** > **Weak**

## Key mechanics the video leans on

- **Kill credit:** some of the best enchants (Leeching, Refreshment, Exploding, Guarding Strike,
  Rampaging, Busy Bee) only trigger when the *weapon itself* lands the killing blow. Enchants that
  deal their own damage (Swirling, Shockwave, Poison Cloud, Fire Aspect...) "steal" kills and
  switch those off. This is why damage-over-time enchants rank low.
- **Flat vs multiplier damage:** "flat" boosts (Sharpness, Power, Artifact Synergy, Strength potion)
  *add* together, so each extra one matters less. "Multipliers" (Committed, Critical Hit,
  Unchanting) multiply each other. Flat boosts still multiply Dynamo, which is why Dynamo builds
  want them.
- **Roll meta (strongest build):** Burst Bowstring + Cooldown Shot + Light Feather artifact =
  every roll fires 3 charged arrows, each cutting 1.5 s off all artifact cooldowns, which re-arms
  Light Feather (3 s) -> effectively infinite artifacts.
- **"Glue Strike" glitch:** with ranged Void Strike, Weakening (or a Strength potion) stops the
  void bonus from resetting on hit, so it keeps climbing -> roughly a permanent x2 melee damage.
- **Damage reduction stacks multiplicatively**, so Protection's -15% is only ~5% effective on a
  build that already has other reductions.
- **Tower** runs at about power 70-100, normal Apocalypse, few enchanted mobs: Unchanting and
  Cool Down are weaker there; bees, Life Boost, Dynamo and healing are stronger.

## Numbers the video gives that differ from the wiki

- Rush: creator thinks it lasts ~4 s (wiki: 1 s).
- Snowball: described as stunning for 2 s, retriggering every 1 s at III.
- Dynamo: ~660,000 flat damage per stack at max power, up to 20 stacks.
- Soul Siphon III: 20% chance for 5 souls (matches wiki).
