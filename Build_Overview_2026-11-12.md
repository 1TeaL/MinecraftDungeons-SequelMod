# Builds the weapon bonuses make possible (2026-11-12)

What each build needs, which weapons get there **from their own built-ins and bonuses** (past power 316), and what's
missing. Armor and artifacts can add to any build; this only looks at what the weapon gives by itself. Counts =
how many weapons get that enchantment as a bonus. Per-item rows: [Weapon_Builds_2026-11-12.md](Weapon_Builds_2026-11-12.md).

**Strong** = several weapons, melee and ranged. **Thin** = one or two weapons, or one side only. **Missing** = no
weapon builds it.

## Built (2026-11-12)

- **Fire Aspect on bows:** its arrows set the mobs they hit on fire (3 s, the game's burning look); burning mobs lose
  the fire share of their max health, and *Ignite* works on them. It rolls on bows now, and on a melee weapon your
  arrows burn too. The Firebolt Thrower gets Fire Aspect, *Ignite*, Multishot.
- **Shock Web:** mobs a beam touches lose 1 / 2 / 3% of their max health (at most twice a second; Lightning Focus
  boosts it) and are charged for *Overload*. Its flat damage is back to the game's. Guardian Bow: Shock Web,
  *Overload*, Power; Webbed Bow (Shock Web built in): *Overload*, Supercharge.
