# Every weapon: bonuses and the build they make (2026-11-12)

**Built in** = what the item has by itself (gilded / unique). **Bonuses** = the extra built-in enchantments it gains
past power 316, in the order they unlock. Fire Aspect on a bow: its arrows set mobs on fire (new); Shock Web's beams take a share of max health and charge mobs for *Overload* (new). At A+100 (power 365) every weapon also gains **Unchanting**. "2nd" = a
second copy of an enchantment it already has (copies add up). New enchantments are in *italics*.
