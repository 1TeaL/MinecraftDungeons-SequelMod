Sequel Mod

A rebalance for Minecraft Dungeons that makes more builds worth playing, and Endless Apocalypse: Apocalypse Plus all the way to A+100, with rewards past the gear cap.

v1.0 · 21 October 2026 · Steam version on Windows

Download the mod · 8 MB

SequelMod-1.0.zip on GitHub, everything included

What it does

  • Endless Apocalypse. A+26 to A+100. Gear power climbs to the cap of 315 by A+38; past that, levels unlock bonus enchantments, extra armor perks, a final enchantment at A+100 and an endless Sharpness / Power / Protection ladder after it.
  • Rebalanced gear. Weak weapons, bows, enchantments, artifacts and armor perks brought up to the strong ones. Every unique beats its base item.
  • Seasonal uniques are back. They drop in missions and show up at merchants, each with a build of its own.
  • Fairer mobs. Enchanted mobs no longer roll Deflect or Levitation Shot.
  • Less grind in camp. Blacksmith upgrades take one mission, merchants stock gear at your power level, no slowdown after a roll.

Install

  1. Download the zip above.
  2. In Steam, right-click Minecraft Dungeons, then Manage > Browse local files.
  3. Extract the zip into that folder. You get Mods\SequelMod and Play Sequel Mod.bat next to the game.
  4. Start the game with Play Sequel Mod.bat and keep its black window open while you play.

Good to know

  • Nothing else to install. The launcher brings its own Python.
  • Multiplayer: everyone in the party needs the mod, started the same way.
  • Your saves are backed up at every start, to Mods\SequelMod\save_backups.
  • Steam Deck: the stat changes work; Endless and the enchantment rewards need Windows for now.
  • Uninstall: delete Mods\SequelMod and Dungeons\Content\Paks\~mods\SequelMod_P.pak.

Endless Apocalypse (Apocalypse +26 to +100)

  • Apocalypse Plus goes to +100 (base game: +25). A+1-25 are unchanged.
  • Gear power: about 254 at A+25, the gear strength cap (315) at A+38, then 1 more per level up to 365 at A+100. Above 315 gear gets no stronger by itself; the levels unlock the rewards below.

Rewards above the gear cap

  • Bonus built-in enchantments: from power 316, every 4 power adds one tier - first the item's own built-ins rise to tier 3, then bonus enchantments from its list below, each up to tier 3. An item gets as many bonuses as bring it to 4 enchantments beyond its family's plain item: three for a unique with one enchantment of its own, two for one with two (a plain item gets three). At power 365 everything is tier 3 and weapons get one more: Unchanting for every melee weapon, the next name of its list for the others.
  • An item type's bonuses follow its best copy; weaker copies of the same item get them too.
  • Enchantments the item already has are skipped, unless the list says second copy; the last entry of a list is a spare for that.
  • Past power 365: every 5 power adds one tier of Sharpness (melee), Power (ranged), Protection (armor) as new lines under the other enchantments (I, II, III, then another line). Drops at power 365 or more come in 1-3 above your power level, so the number keeps climbing.
  • Armor: one more perk at power 330, 345, 360 - the first perks of its list below that it doesn't have yet.
  • New drops of power 257 or more have a 20% chance to be gilded (tier I / II / III: 50% / 35% / 15%).
  • Merchant stock follows your gear power above the base game's ceiling.
